Why journals fail

A blank page on a Tuesday night is a terrible way to remember your life.

Most journalling apps hand you an empty box and hope you feel inspired. You don't, so you stop — and the year disappears anyway. Memoires starts from the opposite end: from the thousands of photos you already took, which are a far better record of what actually happened than your memory of a Tuesday.

How it works

It notices first. You just write.

  1. 1

    Memoires looks through your photos

    In the background, entirely on your iPhone, it groups recent photos by time and place to spot a weekend away, a birthday, a day that clearly wasn't ordinary. Nothing is uploaded to do this.

  2. 2

    It nudges you at the right moment

    A quiet local notification: you haven't written about Lisbon yet. Plus a daily reminder if you want one. Scheduled on your device — no push server involved.

  3. 3

    The entry is half-written already

    You open it and the photos, the date and the place are waiting. Type, or dictate it out loud while you're walking. Two minutes and the memory is kept.

Features

Everything you need to keep the habit

Trip & event detection

The part no other journal does. Memoires watches for clusters of photos that look like something happened — a trip, a party, a long day out — and surfaces them as entries waiting to be written. The analysis happens on your iPhone, including in the background, and your photos are never uploaded.

Photo-led entries

Pull in shots straight from your camera roll, or take one in the app. The picture carries the entry.

Voice dictation

Talk instead of typing. For most languages the transcription runs on your device, so the audio goes nowhere.

Refine with AI

Tidy up a rambling entry into something readable. Optional, premium, and only ever on the one entry you tap it for.

Timeline & calendar

Scroll your life as a stream, or step back and see it laid out month by month.

Mood & place

Tag how the day felt and where it happened. Location is stored inside the entry, on your device — never sent to a server.

Find any memory

Search across everything you've written — a name, a city, a feeling — and land straight on the day.

Locked, and yours

Face ID on the app if you want it, and optional iCloud sync across your own devices.

Privacy

A journal you can be honest in.

Reading someone's photo library is a lot to ask. So the rule is simple: the scanning happens on your iPhone and the results stay there. We built the business on subscriptions, not on you.

Read the full privacy policy
Stored on your device
Entries, photos, moods and places live locally in the app. iCloud sync is off until you turn it on.
Photos are never uploaded
Not for scanning, not for suggestions, not for anything. They do not leave your iPhone.
One clearly-marked exception
Refine with AI sends the text of that one entry to be rewritten, and only when you tap it. Nothing else goes with it.
No ads, no profiling
No advertising, no selling data, no tracking you across other apps. Anonymous usage stats can be switched off.

In development

We're working on connecting Memoires to photo album printers, so the year you've been quietly writing can come back as an object you can hold — once a year, or after a trip worth keeping on a shelf. A journal that ends up on paper is a journal that outlives the phone it was written on.
