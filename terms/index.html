Description of Service

Memoires is a personal journal application for iOS that allows you to create, store, and organize journal entries with text, photos, voice dictation, mood tracking, and location tagging. It can also scan your photo library on your device to suggest days you have not written about, and, for subscribers, rewrite an entry using an AI model. The App stores your data locally on your device and optionally syncs through your personal iCloud account.
